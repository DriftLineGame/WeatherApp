Data sources

Forecast & current conditions come from Open-Meteo, which picks the best national weather models for your spot. In Canada that includes Environment Canada's high-resolution GEM/HRDPS model. Licensed CC BY 4.0.
Radar (ECCC) is the 1 km North American radar composite from Environment and Climate Change Canada's MSC GeoMet, refreshed every few minutes and covering Canada and the US. Open Government Licence – Canada.
Radar (Global) is the backup for places outside North America: weather data by RainViewer (free tier, lower zoom).
Weather alerts are official warnings, watches and statements from Environment Canada's alerts API (Canadian locations only).
Air quality comes from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API (CAMS model data). Place search uses Open-Meteo Geocoding (GeoNames), and "use my location" names come from BigDataCloud.
Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO. Map engine: Leaflet.
Forecasts are model output and can be wrong. For safety decisions, check weather.gc.ca.
